Net EPA = offense EPA/play minus defense EPA/play allowed; the gap shows which team has been better per play. Prop matchups

Players the value board projects above their season average this week, biggest projection first; "new role" marks a projection 1.5 times his average or more (usually a new starter). Board is its projection for this game: his recent usage, the expected game script and the opponent, with the matchup counted at the size that has held up in testing. A defense's position split is mostly noise: about a quarter of a WR split and a tenth of a TE split carries into the next game, so Opp allows (raw, not adjusted for schedule) is context, not a multiplier. Line is the books' main line (DraftKings and FanDuel). vs line is the board's projection minus it: a reason to look, not a bet. On their own the board's prop projections are less accurate than the books' lines, which is why the parlay tool prices legs mainly on the books' number.
